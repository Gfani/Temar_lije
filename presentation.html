Introduction to Temar Lije

Temar Lije is an innovative, real-time educational management platform designed to streamline operations across schools, universities, and remote learning groups.

By integrating a responsive web dashboard with low-latency WebSocket communication, Temar Lije enables instantaneous peer-to-peer study sessions, timed assessment tracking, digital material distribution, and strict role-based privacy isolation between educators and student study groups.

Core Ecosystem Pillars
  • Virtual Classrooms: Assignments, lecture notes & audio lectures.
  • Real-Time Collaboration: Instant chat & voice recording.
  • Timed Quiz Engine: Live countdown & auto-grading.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Containerized Docker & Azure VM.

Statement of the Problem

Modern educational institutions face critical operational and collaboration bottlenecks:

❌ Fragmented & Disconnected Communication
Educators and students rely on scattered external chat apps, emails, and physical handouts, resulting in missed deadlines and poor engagement.
❌ Inefficient Manual Assessment
Manual exam creation, paper grading, and delay in returning test feedback drain teacher productivity and slow student learning.

Key Pain Points Identified

• 70% of students report difficulty tracking assignments across multiple platforms.

• Teachers spend over 8 hours weekly on manual paper-based grading.

• Lack of dedicated real-time peer study spaces.

Statement of the Problem (Continued)

Security, privacy, and organizational vulnerabilities in existing learning tools:

🛡️
Lack of Privacy in Student Study Groups
Standard platforms fail to separate public teacher announcements from private peer study groups. Students lack confidential channels for peer review without teacher surveillance.
📁
Unstandardized Material Sharing
Lecture slides, PDF documents, and audio recordings are shared across third-party drives with broken permissions and no organized structure.

Statement of the Problem (Continued)

Operational barriers and attendance verification:

⏱️
Attendance Tracking Friction
Manual attendance sheets waste lecture time, enable proxy check-ins, and lack digital validation against location or Wi-Fi networks.
📊
Limited Real-Time Oversight
Administrators and educators have no consolidated dashboard to track student participation, quiz submission rates, or platform engagement.

Deep Dive 1: Real-Time Chat & Voice Engine

WebSocket Gateway (`chat.gateway.ts`) and Media Streaming:

⚙️ Gateway Architecture
  • `@SubscribeMessage('joinRoom')`: Authenticates user and joins socket room.
  • `@SubscribeMessage('sendMessage')`: Persists message in PostgreSQL and broadcasts `newMessage`.
  • Nginx `Upgrade $http_upgrade` proxy with `86400s` keep-alive.
// NestJS ChatGateway (chat.gateway.ts) @SubscribeMessage('sendMessage') async handleMessage(@ConnectedSocket() client, @MessageBody() data) { const msg = await this.chatService.saveMessage(data); this.server.to(`room_${data.groupId}`).emit('newMessage', msg); }

Deep Dive 2: RBAC Security & Authority

Database-level isolation rules in `ChatService` and `ClassroomsService`:

🔒 Peer Privacy Guard
`getGroups` excludes teachers (`role === 'TEACHER'`) from student peer study groups to maintain confidential peer collaboration.
🗑️ Cascade Deletion
`DELETE /classrooms/:id` verifies teacher ownership and cleanly deletes all materials, quizzes, and messages in a single transaction.
🚫 Account Revocation
Removing a student unlinks all classroom memberships and revokes the student account from the system.
